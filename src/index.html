UX Was Here

Inspired by the playful graffiti mark "Kilroy was here," our journey began with a simple observation in a crowded company cafeteria. Serving approximately 1,500 people every day, lunchtime turned into chaos, as employees scrambled to select food and make payments. The experience was fraught with frustration, inefficiency, and a shared desire for something better.
